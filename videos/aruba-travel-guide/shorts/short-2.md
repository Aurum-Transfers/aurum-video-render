# Aruba: Which Coast to Swim

Pick the wrong Aruba coast and you'll only get photos.
<!-- f80126 -->

Quick note: Aurum links to partner companies and may earn a commission if you book.

The west and south coasts are calm and protected, good for swimming and snorkelling. The east and north coasts face the open sea, with stronger winds and crashing waves.
<!-- f80114 f80115 -->

On the east side, Boca Grandi suits kitesurfing, but its strong current makes it a poor place to swim. For calm water, Baby Beach is the most child-friendly beach on the island, and at Arashi Beach the best snorkelling is on the left as you face the sea.
