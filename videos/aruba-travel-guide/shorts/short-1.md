# Aruba: Do This Before You Fly

Don't board a flight to Aruba without this.
<!-- f80267 -->

Quick note: Aurum links to partner companies and may earn a commission if you book.

Every traveller to Aruba, including infants and children, must complete the online ED-Card and get it approved. You can fill it in within seven days before your trip.
<!-- f80267 f80268 -->

The airline asks for proof of approval before you fly, and even passengers in transit need a card. Bring a valid passport, and check the tourism authority's traveller requirements page for the current sustainability fee details.
